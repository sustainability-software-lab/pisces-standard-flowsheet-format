# Contributions

## Authors & Acknowledgements

[PISCES-SFF](https://github.com/sustainability-software-lab/pisces-standard-flowsheet-format) was originally created and developed by [Sarang S. Bhagwat](https://sarangbhagwat.com/) as part of the [Scown Group](https://cscown.com/), the [Energy & Biosciences Institute](https://energybiosciencesinstitute.org/) at the [University of California, Berkeley](https://www.berkeley.edu/), as well as the [Joint BioEnergy Institute](https://www.jbei.org/) at [Lawrence Berkeley National Lab](https://www.lbl.gov/). Sarang continues to lead PISCES-SFF developments and maintains the repository.

The PISCES-SFF package (which includes the Standard Flowsheet Format, validator, and BioSTEAM exporter) is consumed by [Project PISCES](https://projectpisces.org/) (Process Integration & Synthesis using Chemical Engineering Standards).

Here is a growing list of core contributers who have committed significant
resources, expertise, and other impactful efforts:

* [Sarang Bhagwat](https://sarangbhagwat.com/) (lead developer and maintainer)

* [Corinne Scown](https://eta.lbl.gov/people/corinne-scown) (led efforts to found, develop, coordinate, and expand the software)

* [Tyler Huntington](https://github.com/tylerhuntington) (integrated PISCES-SFF with the Project PISCES website and contributed improvements to metadata representation)

* [Yuting Chen](https://eta.lbl.gov/people/yuting-chen) (built the [LLM-based extraction pipeline](https://projectpisces.org/?page=extract-pdf) in Project PISCES and contributed general expertise through discussions)

* [Meili Gong](https://github.com/mglbleta) (contributed general expertise through discussions)
